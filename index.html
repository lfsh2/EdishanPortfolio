Case Study 1: Multi-System Lead Qualification Automation

The Problem

A service business was manually reviewing 200+ inbound leads per week, spending 3-4 hours daily on initial qualification, data entry across three systems (CRM, scheduling tool, email platform), and routing leads to sales reps.

The System Built

Implemented an n8n workflow integrated with GoHighLevel that:

  • Captured leads from web forms and automatically enriched contact data using external APIs
  • Applied AI-based scoring to qualify leads based on industry, company size, and engagement signals
  • Routed qualified leads into GoHighLevel pipelines with appropriate tags and assigned sales reps based on territory and capacity
  • Triggered personalized email sequences and scheduled follow-up tasks automatically
  • Logged all activity to a central dashboard with error notifications for failed steps

Operational Impact

  • Reduced manual lead review time from 20 hours/week to 3 hours/week
  • Increased lead response speed from 4-6 hours to under 5 minutes
  • Eliminated data entry errors across systems through automated syncing
  • Enabled sales team to focus on high-value conversations instead of administrative tasks

Stack: n8n · OpenAI (structured-output qualification) · Webhooks · CRM integration · Gmail · Google Calendar · Google Sheets

Case Study 2: Teethly.ph - Dental Marketplace & Clinic Platform

The Problem

Patients in the Philippines had no reliable way to find a verified dentist nearby, compare options, and get in touch without phone tag. On the other side, clinics were running on legacy desktop software with no online presence, no online booking, and no way to attract new patients - while losing time to manual scheduling, no-shows, and paper records.

The System Built

A two-sided platform built with Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui, deployed on Vercel:

  • Patient marketplace: search by location and service (toothache, cleaning, braces, whitening, kids, wisdom tooth), PRC-verified dentist profiles with real reviews, and three ways to connect - chat, video call, or booking - free for patients, plus iOS and Android apps
  • Clinic operating system: smart scheduling with SMS reminders and double-booking prevention, a patient CRM with an interactive odontogram and immutable clinical notes, e-prescription PDF generation, billing and revenue tracking in pesos, automated review requests, and role-based staff access
  • Business model built in: a free clinic tier, a Pro tier with a 30-day no-card trial, and a pay-per-new-patient fee that only charges when the platform delivers a lead
  • Privacy by design: encrypted records, per-clinic data isolation with access controls, and compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

Outcome

  • Live at teethly.ph, launched in Davao with rollout to Cebu, Metro Manila, and more cities
  • Replaces four separate tools for a clinic - marketplace listing, booking, patient records, and billing - with one system
  • Founding-clinic program live: first 20 clinics get new-patient leads free for a year
  • Shipped as a marketing site, patient web app, clinic dashboard, and mobile apps from a single codebase and design system

Stack: Next.js (App Router) · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · shadcn/ui · Lucide Icons · Vercel

Automation Engineering

GoHighLevel & n8n Automation Capabilities

I design and implement backend automation systems that handle complex business logic while remaining maintainable over time. My approach focuses on building workflows with flexible, changeable logic layers on top of locked-down core processes—ensuring reliability while allowing business rules to evolve without breaking existing integrations.

Core Automation Capabilities

CRM Automation & Pipeline Management

Built multi-stage pipeline automations in GoHighLevel with conditional logic for lead routing, automatic tagging based on behavior, and scheduled follow-up sequences. Designed workflows that trigger different paths based on lead source, engagement level, and qualification criteria.

Webhook & API Integrations

Developed custom webhook handlers to sync data between GoHighLevel and external systems (payment processors, scheduling tools, custom databases). Implemented bidirectional syncs with error handling, retry logic, and logging to ensure no data loss during network failures or API rate limits.

Multi-Step Workflow Automation

Created complex n8n, Make.com, and Zapier workflows that orchestrate actions across 5+ platforms. Examples include lead capture → enrichment → qualification → CRM entry → notification → follow-up email sequences, all running without manual intervention.

Error Handling & Reliability Design

Implemented retry mechanisms for failed API calls, fallback paths for missing data, and monitoring alerts for workflow failures. Designed systems to gracefully degrade rather than break entirely when third-party services go down.

Systems Design Philosophy

I build automation systems with three architectural principles:

  • Flexible Logic Layer: Business rules (tags, routing decisions, timing) live in easy-to-change configuration or separate modules, not buried in core workflow code.
  • Locked-Down Core Workflows: Critical processes like payment handling, data syncing, and authentication follow strict, tested patterns that don't change unless absolutely necessary.
  • Long-Term Maintainability: Every automation is documented with clear trigger conditions, expected inputs/outputs, and known failure points. Systems are designed so someone else (or future me) can understand and modify them six months later.
